# HALO Party Team Operating Standard

This charter defines how the Halo Party Team operates `/live-party/` so event quality, loyalty perks, and access-tier decisions stay consistent and auditable.

## Mission and scope

- Keep the internal-first Live Party Hub reliable, welcoming, and worth returning to.
- Preserve the free music base while offering optional supporter and VIP upgrades.
- Run gift/unlock/perk mechanics that feel earned and improve participation quality.
- Ship and operate seasonal events without ad hoc decisions or unclear ownership.

In scope: party experience standards, event programming, party-specific perk packaging, access-tier messaging quality, launch readiness, and post-event quality review.

Out of scope: replacing core moderation authority, payment-state truth, or competition governance.

## Team roles and responsibilities

### Party experience / atmosphere

- **Owner:** Experience Owner
- Defines room tone, host prompts, Dreamweave cue quality, and fan-floor pacing.
- Maintains "internal room first" standard for all party launches.

### Gamification and loyalty perks

- **Owner:** Loyalty & Perks Owner
- Maps party behaviors to healthy progression (attendance, constructive participation, return visits).
- Reviews perk balance with the Gamification Team before launch.

### Gifts / unlocks / rewards

- **Owner:** Loyalty & Perks Owner + Party Lead
- Approves gift and unlock moments used in party events.
- Requires every reward to have an earned trigger and a clear player-facing explanation.

### Access tier standards

- **Owner:** Access Standards Owner
- Enforces clear Free / Supporter / VIP boundaries in event briefs and user messaging.
- Blocks launches that hide core music access behind premium routing.

### Moderation / safety / escalation boundaries

- **Owner:** Moderation Liaison
- Uses existing community actions (`message`, `reaction`, `report`) and moderation pathways.
- Escalates safety incidents immediately to Community moderation owners; Party Team does not bypass trust boundaries.

### Event launch / seasonal programming

- **Owner:** Seasonal Programming Owner
- Owns event calendar, campaign parameters, seasonal hooks, and run-of-show checklist completion.
- Confirms optional external relay setup never replaces the internal venue.

### Analytics / quality checks

- **Owner:** QA / Analytics Owner
- Reviews participation quality, perk usage, unlock completion, and report-rate signals after every launch.
- Opens corrective follow-up when quality or safety signals drift.

## Operating principles and standards

1. **Free music base is permanent:** core listening and baseline room participation remain free.
2. **Premium must be explicit:** every gated perk must state what is gated, why it is gated, and which tier unlocks it.
3. **Rewards must be earned:** no manipulative dark patterns, artificial scarcity pressure, or unclear chance mechanics.
4. **Safety boundaries are non-negotiable:** moderation/reporting pathways stay intact at all times.
5. **Internal-first venue:** external streaming is optional relay distribution, never the primary party venue.
6. **No shadow payment logic:** party flows consume approved access/tier state and do not invent new billing truth.

## Launch and operations playbook

Use this checklist for each launch window.

### A) Pre-launch (go/no-go)

- [ ] Event brief names host, theme, season, and intended audience.
- [ ] Free-core experience is defined and publicly visible in launch copy.
- [ ] Supporter/VIP perks are listed with explicit tier, rationale, and fallback behavior if hooks are absent.
- [ ] Gift/unlock criteria are earned, understandable, and non-manipulative.
- [ ] Moderation coverage and escalation contacts are confirmed.
- [ ] Optional external relay destinations are configured as distribution only.
- [ ] QA confirms contract checks and launch-readiness criteria are green.

### B) During event (live operations)

- [ ] Monitor room health (messages, reports, engagement quality) in real time.
- [ ] Keep host prompts aligned with atmosphere and safety boundaries.
- [ ] Apply tier gating exactly as published; no surprise mid-event restrictions.
- [ ] Escalate moderation issues immediately using existing community pathways.

### C) Post-event (quality loop)

- [ ] Capture event outcomes: attendance, constructive participation, perk usage, and report trends.
- [ ] Record what worked, what drifted, and what changes are required before next launch.
- [ ] Route cross-team fixes (Community, Gamification, Payments, Campaign tooling) with clear owners and deadlines.

## Change control

- Charter changes require Party Lead review plus Site Leadership approval when they affect cross-team boundaries.
- Any change that impacts free-vs-premium behavior must be reflected in launch copy and contract checks before release.
