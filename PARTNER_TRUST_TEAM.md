# HALO Partner Trust Team

The Partner Trust Team is an owner-only desk for transparent communication with the platforms HALO uses or hopes to work with. It keeps the intended product use, recorded safeguards, drafted messages, human approvals, sharing history, and partner responses in one auditable place.

The team never sends email, submits forms, posts messages, schedules contact, accepts terms, or represents that a partnership exists. It prepares a brief for the owner, and the owner chooses whether and how to share it through an official channel.

## Team roles

- **Bridge** explains why HALO is reaching out and makes one clear request.
- **Covenant** converts responsible-use promises into controls that can be checked.
- **Rights** keeps intended use aligned with plan rights and third-party permissions.
- **Signal** describes the real technical workflow without hiding or overstating automation.
- **Mirror** removes unsupported relationships, absolute promises, pressure, and invented facts.

## Suno starting record

The initial Suno record uses the public HALO account supplied by the owner on August 14, 2026. It describes HALO's intended workflow as generating original loops, transitions, stems, atmospheres, vocal textures, and soundscapes, then transforming permitted downloads through human-led DJ arrangement, effects, mixing, recording, and release review.

Its safeguards require rights records for each asset, policy review before scaling, no artist imitation, no unlicensed third-party inputs, no scraping or limit bypass, no credential sharing, no unofficial automation, and human approval for every external message and commercial release. Suno remains marked as a prospective platform relationship unless Suno confirms otherwise.

## Operating flow

1. The owner records a platform, the source of the contact information, intended use, and concrete safeguards.
2. The team drafts an introduction, usage disclosure, policy question, or partnership conversation from that recorded evidence.
3. The owner approves or archives the draft. Approval still does not send anything.
4. The owner copies the approved brief into a verified official channel and records that it was shared.
5. Any response is recorded so future briefs reflect the actual relationship rather than assumptions.

The desk is available at `/partner-trust.html`, and its owner-only API is `/api/partner-trust`.
