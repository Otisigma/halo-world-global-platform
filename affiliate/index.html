Program terms

  • 10% recurring share of eligible subscription revenue.
  • Up to 12 active paid months per attributed referral, not 12 calendar months or 12 webhook deliveries.
  • $50 USD minimum accrued before payout; held or reversed amounts cannot be treated as available.
  • 14-day refund/chargeback hold before earnings become available. Passing the hold does not initiate a payout.

Eligible revenue must come from verified, successfully paid subscription invoices linked to a valid, consented referral. No such pipeline exists here yet. Eligible products, treatment of taxes, discounts and fees, partial payments, billing intervals, and rounding still require an approved server-side definition before any accrual. Mix purchases, licenses, invitation joins, and access flags are not proof of eligible subscription revenue.

Production prerequisites & deployment boundaries
  • Deploy the Netlify function at /api/affiliate-dashboard with Netlify Identity and the existing database. Self-hosted static serving alone does not provide this private API. No new migration, payment secret, or payout worker is included.
  • Invitation visibility requires the existing 20260819150000_create-tracked-share-invites.sql migration. Verified rewards require 20261019030000_track_drop_notifications.sql. Missing data sources remain unavailable, never synthetic.
  • Before paid attribution: establish explicit tracking consent, one auditable referral owner, self-referral prevention, abuse review, and a verified subscription/customer relationship. Existing share-invite joins alone are insufficient.
  • Before accrual: add signed payment-event verification and an immutable or adjustment-based money ledger with source invoice/payment IDs, referral ownership, currency, active-month accounting, and unique deduplication keys. Transactions and locking must enforce exactly-once processing under concurrent/retried events, including the 12th/13th-month boundary.
  • Refunds and chargebacks need linked, idempotent negative adjustments, including partial refunds and post-payout recovery. Release holds only after 14 days; apply the $50 USD threshold to reconciled payout eligibility. Do not delete or silently rewrite earnings.
  • Before payouts: implement affiliate-specific Connect onboarding, trusted tax/KYC/review status, currency and transfer-capability checks, explicit operational/configuration gates, durable batch claims, Stripe idempotency, and reconciliation before marking individual ledger entries paid. Test retry, concurrent, failure, and test-mode scenarios before enabling monetary movement. The separate music royalty service is not an affiliate payout worker.
  • Before Guide awards: approve the Signal-to-member-ledger mapping and persist tier authorization plus versioned, server-enforced multipliers without retroactively changing existing points.
