The charter

THE ROLE HAS WEIGHT.
IT DOES NOT CREATE A HIERARCHY.

01

Creators first

Help artists retain agency, find useful resources, and reach the right people without speaking over them.

02

Care for the room

Welcome new members, model respectful conduct, and escalate concerns instead of improvising authority.

03

Make work visible

Share practical contributions honestly. The title follows service; service never exists to chase the title.

04

Remain accountable

Human review grants the role, records decisions, and can close a term when trust or participation changes.
