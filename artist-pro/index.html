STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you apply.

Does HALO replace my distributor?

No. HALO helps prepare, organise, present, measure, and learn from the release. Distribution remains with the service the artist chooses.

Does Artist Pro guarantee radio play or press?

No. HALO can provide structured consideration, a stronger release pack, and better-informed outreach preparation. Editorial and programming decisions remain independent.

Does the AI contact people or publish for me?

No. It reads permitted HALO signals, prepares plans and drafts, and waits. The artist or authorised human team approves and performs every external action.

What does HALO keep confidential?

Private artist information, unreleased work, credentials, payment details, internal notes, proprietary prompts, and operational security controls are not made public through Artist Pro. HALO shares only what is required to deliver an approved action.

Can I apply before the song is finished?

Yes. Recording and finishing stages are often the best time to build rights, versions, campaign facts, and release timing correctly.
