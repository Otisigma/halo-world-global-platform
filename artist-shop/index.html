Proof & Provenance

Every signal. Every master. Verified.

Follow the complete journey of every track from studio master to real-world play. Our Proof and Provenance system pairs autonomous signal tracking with cryptographic master verification so fans and creators can trace authenticity, track release milestones, and experience music backed by transparent origin records.

  • Master Provenance: cryptographic verification tying releases back to their original master source.
  • Signal Tracking: continuous observation across broadcasts, streams, and listener activity.
  • Complete Music Journey: connect directly with the artist's catalog, verified releases, and behind-the-scenes lineage.
