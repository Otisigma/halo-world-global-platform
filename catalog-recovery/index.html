A register is not a backup. A stamp is not legal ownership.

The recovery pack includes only files you select. ZIP downloads are not encrypted. Store the pack on encrypted storage, keep copies in separate locations, and arrange authorized access with your solicitor. Never enter passwords, seed phrases or private keys here. Private keys are not needed to recover audio.

What should my solicitor or trusted custodian receive?

Provide the encrypted archive and printed inventory, plus references to agreements and your legal instructions. Keep access secrets separately through an agreed secure arrangement. Review collaborator shares, samples, licences, inheritance and account access with your solicitor. HALO does not transfer rights, assign a monetary value or guarantee recovery. A normal USB is storage, not a hardware signing wallet.
