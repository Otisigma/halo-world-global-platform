Proposed model / Open for discussion

HOW HALO CAN EARN
WITHOUT OWNING THE ARTIST.

The business model is not a secret deal and it is not final. These are the routes being discussed. Exact fees, splits, payout timing, and responsibilities must be written clearly and agreed with artists before launch.

01

A clear service fee

If HALO helps sell an approved release, tool, class, or experience, HALO can earn a disclosed platform fee. The artist sees the price, costs, split, and expected payout before choosing to participate.

02

Optional fan membership

Fans can pay HALO for extra platform features and community access. Artist ownership is not the product, and membership does not give HALO rights to an artist’s work.

03

Disclosed partnerships

HALO can earn from relevant sponsors or affiliate recommendations when the relationship is labelled clearly and does not change an artist’s rights or force a purchase.

What is not happening now

No artist contract is created by viewing this site. No music is licensed, sold, uploaded, or transferred by this preview. The concept profiles and catalog are demonstrations until real artists approve their participation and rights are reviewed.
