Production layer

Build one master show

Mix DJ audio, microphones, cameras, graphics, sponsor moments, and captions in a dedicated encoder. Keep a clean recording locally for clips and replay.

  • Landscape master for YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, and HALO
  • Vertical-safe camera composition for TikTok and Instagram
  • Separate music, microphone, and guest audio controls

Distribution layer

Relay, monitor, recover

Connect eligible platform accounts inside a reputable multistream provider. Keep stream keys and credentials there—not in this public website—and monitor every destination during the show.

  • Test with unlisted or private destinations first
  • Assign one producer to quality and one host to fans
  • Keep a backup internet path and local recording
