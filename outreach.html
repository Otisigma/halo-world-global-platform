HOW THIS DESK BEHAVES

        
The rules are in the database, not in a prompt

        
    
          
  • An approach cannot be stored as approved or sent without the member id of the person who approved it.
    • 
          
  • Each contact receives at most one approach per release, enforced by a unique constraint.
    • 
          
  • A contact who opts out is withdrawn from every queue immediately, and cannot be approved afterwards.
    • 
          
  • Each contact carries their own frequency cap, and the cap is re-checked at the moment of writing.
    • 
          
  • An approach that cites no real fact about the record or the contact is dropped before you see it.
    • 
          
  • HALO holds no mail credentials. Every approach here is sent by you, from your own mail.
