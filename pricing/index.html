Before your next step

Can I subscribe today?

Not from this preview. Explore the public music experience now, or ask Halo about a proposed paid plan. Availability and terms must be confirmed before any purchase.

Are these separate plans or add-ons?

Creator and Pro Creator are proposed music memberships. Shield Plus is a proposed business protection package. Enterprise is scoped individually. Bundles, entitlements, and any add-on pricing will be agreed before launch.

How do I request access or a demo?

Open the Halo feedback desk, sign in, select “I need help,” and include the plan name, your team size, and what you want to achieve. Ask for access or a demo; do not send payment details.
