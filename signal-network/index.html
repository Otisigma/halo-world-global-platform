Platform Modules

Six rooms.
One world.

Every module inside Signal Network is built to give artists a specific kind of control over a specific part of their creative life.

Module 01

Sovereign Vault

A private, encrypted archive for everything that belongs to you. Tracks, stems, artwork, contracts, credits, split sheets, release notes, and the full history of every decision. Nothing leaves without your sign-off.

Private by default
  • Full catalogue storage — released and unreleased
  • Version control for stems, mixes, and masters
  • Split and rights documentation
  • Release history and decision log
  • Artwork and metadata archive
  • Contract and agreement storage
  • Granular access controls per asset

Module 02

Pearl Hall

A visual timeline of every milestone, release, win, and important moment in your career. Each Pearl represents a real creative achievement — something you can revisit, share privately, or build on.

  • Milestone timeline for every release
  • First-idea-to-launch story for each track
  • Collaboration credits and acknowledgements
  • Private and shareable Pearl views
Live milestone archive

Module 03

Dreamweaver

An intelligence layer that turns raw creative material into useful outputs. Feed in a session, a voice note, a rough draft, or an idea — and Dreamweaver helps shape it into release copy, campaign language, next-step plans, and creative prompts.

  • Session-to-summary synthesis
  • Release copy and bio drafts
  • Campaign language and pitch material
  • Creative prompts from existing work
AI-assisted, artist-directed

Module 04

Ritual Engine

A simple daily focus loop built for creative momentum. Ritual Engine takes the chaos out of a working day and replaces it with a clear, repeatable sequence that keeps releases moving forward.

  • Capture — log what happened today
  • Refine — move one thing forward
  • Publish — put something into the world
  • Review — see what is working
  • Repeat — build the habit
Daily creative practice

Module 05

Release Desk

A single control room for everything a release needs before it goes live. Metadata, artwork, rights, credits, distribution, launch sequencing — all in one place so nothing is missing when it matters.

  • Metadata completion and ISRC management
  • Artwork review and approval workflow
  • Rights and credits verification
  • Distribution routing and launch scheduling
Release-ready checklist

Module 06

Signal Network

A live view of creative momentum — what is gaining traction, what needs attention, and what should happen next. Signal Network connects every other module and surfaces the signals that matter most to you right now.

  • Release health and traction overview
  • Collaborator activity and open actions
  • Catalogue-wide momentum signals
  • Prioritised next-step recommendations
Live intelligence layer

Trusted Circles

Every person
in the right place.

Signal Network uses layered access so your creative life stays organized and private. Three concentric circles — each with its own permissions, visibility, and purpose.

Artist Core

The innermost circle. Full access to the Sovereign Vault, all release materials, earnings data, and every creative decision. This is your private space — no one enters without your explicit invitation.

Creative Team

Producers, managers, collaborators, and designers. Access is scoped to the specific projects, stems, briefs, and approvals they need. Feedback flows in — final decisions flow out from the core.

Extended World

Labels, distributors, press contacts, and legal partners. This circle receives only what they need to do their job — delivery-ready assets, approved bios, release schedules, and nothing more.
